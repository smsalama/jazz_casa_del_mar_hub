## Open questions

These are ambiguities, gaps or contradictions found in the brief. None has been resolved by the analyst. Where a requirement below could not be written without a working answer, the requirement carries the marker **Provisional (OQ-n)** and the acceptance criteria that depend on it carry the same marker in `docs/stories.md`. A provisional criterion must be re-checked when the question is answered.

The first three are the open questions already in the brief (brief numbers 1 to 3, tracked as Q8 to Q10 in `state/project.json`). OQ-4 onward are new.

**OQ-1 Admin users and permission levels** (brief open question 1, Q8). How many staff will have admin accounts, and do they need different permission levels (for example Editor, who can edit content, versus Administrator, who can also manage accounts, read enquiries and export subscribers)? Affects FR-34, FR-42 to FR-44 and NFR-25. Working answer: a small team of fewer than 10 accounts and two roles, Administrator and Editor, as set out in FR-34. Owner: Steven.

**OQ-2 Enquiry notification by email** (brief open question 2, Q9). Should a new enquiry also be emailed to staff (this needs the client's own mail server and its credentials), or should enquiries only appear in the admin portal? Affects FR-21, FR-34 (password reset by email) and NFR-31 (cost). Working answer: admin portal only is mandatory; email notification is written as an optional, off by default feature (FR-21). Owner: Steven.

**OQ-3 Default language and language detection** (brief open question 3, Q10). Which of English, German, Italian and Russian is the default language, and is the language chosen automatically from the visitor's browser settings? Affects FR-24, FR-25 and NFR-28. Working answer: English is the default, and the first visit to the site root chooses a language from the browser's preferred languages, falling back to English. Owner: Steven.

**OQ-4 Meaning of "edit everything" in the admin portal.** Steven asked to "edit everything including all elements, titles and everything on the website". Does this cover only text, titles, images, links, list items (rooms, venues, FAQ, offers, gallery) and site settings, or also: creating or deleting whole pages, changing page layout and the order of sections, changing navigation structure, changing colours and fonts, and changing the content or parameters of the 3D scenes (for example scene colours, models, camera paths)? Affects FR-35, FR-36, FR-39 and the architecture (a layout editor is a very different build from a content editor). Working answer: the first list only (text, titles, images, links, list items, settings) within the fixed page templates; the second list is not required until answered. Owner: Steven.

**OQ-5 Editing versus concept marking and noindex.** "Edit everything" conflicts with the decision that every page is marked as a concept and set not to be indexed. May the admin portal be able to remove or empty the concept notice, or switch off noindex? Affects FR-17, FR-39, NFR-27. Working answer: the concept notice wording is editable in all four languages but can never be empty or hidden, and there is no admin setting that changes noindex. Owner: Steven, with the UK legal role.

**OQ-6 Publishing workflow and history.** Do staff edits go live as soon as saved, or is there a draft, preview and publish step? Is a revision history with restore needed, and does publishing need a different permission from editing? Affects FR-38. Working answer: a draft and publish step per page with a preview, and a history of the last 10 published versions per page with restore, written as provisional in FR-38. Owner: Steven.

**OQ-7 Behaviour when a translation is missing.** When a field has no German, Italian or Russian text, should the site show the default language text, show a visible marker, hide the item, or should the admin portal block publishing until all four languages are complete? Affects FR-26, FR-37. Working answer: the public site shows the default language text for that field (so no page is ever blank) and the admin portal flags the gap and shows completeness per page; publishing is not blocked. Owner: Steven.

**OQ-8 Source and quality of German, Italian and Russian copy.** The agency writes placeholder copy. Is machine-assisted translation into German, Italian and Russian acceptable for the concept, and must the legal pages (privacy, cookies, terms) be reviewed by a qualified native speaker or lawyer before any use beyond local review? Affects FR-14 to FR-16 and NFR-28. Working answer: machine-assisted translation is acceptable for a local-review concept, every translated string is marked as unreviewed in the content register, and no use beyond local review without review. Owner: Steven, with the UK legal role.

**OQ-9 Meaning of "landing pages".** Steven asked for "3D animations and landing pages". Does "landing pages" mean the section overview pages of the site (Home, Rooms, Dining, Pools and beach, Activities, Location), or separate campaign or offer landing pages (for example one page per offer)? Affects FR-28, FR-1. Working answer: the section overview pages; no separate campaign pages. Owner: Steven.

**OQ-10 Retention periods.** Steven asked for "retention rules" but gave no periods. The schedule in this document (see "Retention schedule") is the analyst's proposal. Are these periods acceptable, and is there a client policy that overrides them? The UK legal role reviews them in Phase 3. Affects FR-45 and NFR-17. Working answer: the proposed schedule, stored as configuration so it can change without a rebuild. Owner: Steven, with the UK legal role.

**OQ-11 Newsletter confirmation and sending.** Is a confirmation email (double opt-in) wanted? That needs the client's mail server (see OQ-2 and OQ-14). Does the site itself send newsletters, or does the client export subscribers (FR-43) and send from their own tool? How does a subscriber unsubscribe when no email can be sent from the site? Affects FR-22, FR-23, FR-43. Working answer: single opt-in with a recorded, explicit consent tick; no newsletter sending from the site; unsubscribe through a public page where the subscriber enters their address (FR-23); double opt-in only if OQ-2 is answered yes. Owner: Steven.

**OQ-12 Data controller and lawful basis.** The privacy notice must name who decides how enquiry and subscriber data is used (the controller), and who handles requests from individuals. For a speculative concept that JAZ has not authorised, the answer is unclear (the agency, Steven, or the client if the concept is adopted). Egyptian data protection law may also apply because the operator is in Egypt. Affects FR-14, NFR-14 and NFR-16. Working answer: the privacy notice uses a clearly marked placeholder controller name and contact until answered. Owner: Steven, with the UK legal role.

**OQ-13 Local review only versus hosting on the client's servers.** The brief says the agency packages the site for local review only, and also says there should be no running costs "once hosted on their servers" and that the hosting questionnaire will be filled in "after release". Will the concept ever be hosted anywhere other than local machines? If it is, further clearance is needed for the JAZ name, the Book now link and the contact form receiving real enquiries. Affects FR-47, NFR-27, NFR-31. Working answer: local review only; every requirement still holds if hosted, and noindex stays on. Owner: Steven, with the UK legal role.

**OQ-14 Server capabilities for the architecture phase.** Phase 4 needs to know what the client's server offers: operating system, which runtimes can be installed, whether a database server is available or the site must use an embedded file database, outgoing mail (SMTP) host, TLS certificates and domain, disk quota, and backup facilities. The hosting questionnaire is due only after release, which is too late for architecture. Affects FR-21, FR-47, FR-48, NFR-10, NFR-31 and NFR-32. Working answer: a single small Linux server (1 CPU, 1 GB memory, 10 GB disk), no database server, no mail server unless OQ-2 is yes, TLS in front of the site. Owner: Steven.

**OQ-15 Book now target.** Which exact URL is the "existing JAZ booking engine" for this resort? Is it acceptable for a concept that JAZ has not authorised to link to the live booking engine, and should visitors see a notice that they are leaving the concept site? Affects FR-11, FR-39. Working answer: one configurable URL, initially the resort's page on the group site (as given in the brief), opening in a new tab, with no interstitial. Owner: Steven, with the UK legal role.

**OQ-16 Contact details and social accounts.** Should the phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, street address and social media links be synthetic placeholders or the real ones? Real values would send concept enquiries and chats to the real resort and imply endorsement. Affects FR-12, FR-19, FR-20. Working answer: synthetic placeholder numbers and an example.invalid style address; social links go to the placeholder values until answered; the Google Maps link points to the public location of the resort in Hurghada. Owner: Steven, with the UK legal role.

**OQ-17 Use of the JAZ name, wordmark and badges.** The current page shows the JAZ wordmark, "JAZ go green" and "Green Star" badges and a Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice badge. The brief says the designer extracts the brand. May the concept use the resort name and a typeset wordmark? Must the badges and award claims be left out, because showing them on an unauthorised concept would assert awards that are not the concept's to claim? Affects FR-17, FR-18, NFR-30. Working answer: the resort name is shown as typeset text, no logo artwork is copied, and no award, certification or review badge is shown. Owner: Steven, with the UK legal role.

**OQ-18 Unknowns carried over from the brief.** The brief lists a currency selector (assumed out, no prices shown) and meetings and events pages (not on the current hotel page) as unknown. Please confirm both are out. Affects FR-1. Working answer: both out. Owner: Steven.

**OQ-19 Factual claims in placeholder copy.** Placeholder copy for a real resort will need facts (number of rooms, opening times, distances, star rating, room sizes). Invented figures could mislead a reader who takes the concept for the real site. May the copy contain invented sample figures, or only qualitative statements? Affects FR-18. Working answer: no numeric facts about the resort other than the counts of items the brief names; no prices, no star rating, no awards, no review quotes. Owner: Steven, with the UK legal role.

## How to read this document

- IDs: functional requirements are FR-n, non-functional requirements are NFR-n, open questions are OQ-n. IDs are permanent: a withdrawn requirement keeps its number and is marked withdrawn.
- "Shall" marks a requirement. Every requirement is written so a person or a test can decide pass or fail without interpretation.
- Source: where the requirement comes from (a brief section, one of Steven's decisions numbered D1 to D7 in the brief's "Decisions from Steven", or "Analyst" where the analyst added it as standard practice; analyst additions are listed in `docs/assumptions.md` so Steven can reject them).
- Verify: T is an automated test, M is a manual test or demonstration by a person, I is an inspection of code, configuration or a document, A is a measurement or analysis. QA adds the actual tests later; this field only says what kind of evidence is expected.
- Provisional (OQ-n): the requirement depends on an open question above.

## Glossary and fixed definitions

- **Four languages**: English (en), German (de), Italian (it) and Russian (ru).
- **Page inventory**: the 21 page types per language listed in FR-1, 84 URLs in total.
- **Public site**: the pages visitors see. **Admin portal**: the staff-only area (FR-32 onwards).
- **Enquiry**: one submission of the Contact Us form. **Subscriber**: one newsletter sign-up record.
- **Concept notice**: the persistent notice defined in FR-17.
- **Reference phone R1**: a mid range Android phone of the 2022 to 2024 generation: Snapdragon 6 or 7 series, Exynos 1380 or Dimensity 7000 series class system on chip, 6 GB memory, 1080 by 2400 pixel display, current stable Chrome. The named example is the Samsung Galaxy A54 5G. **Reference phone R2**: iPhone 12 or later, Safari on iOS 16.4 or later.
- **Lab profile L1**: Chrome stable with the Lighthouse mobile preset: simulated slow 4G (1.6 Mbit/s down, 150 ms round trip), 4 times CPU slowdown, cold cache. Budgets marked "R1 or L1" are measured on R1 when the device is available and always on L1; if the two disagree the worse result counts.
- **3D mode**: the page is showing the real-time WebGL scenes. **Fallback mode**: the page is showing the static, non-WebGL presentation defined in NFR-12.
- **Mid range phone class**: R1 and R2 as defined above.
- **Synthetic data**: invented data that cannot identify a real person, for example `visitor.one@example.com`, `Test Visitor`, `+44 20 7946 0000`.

## Functional requirements

### Public site content and navigation

**FR-1 Page inventory.** The public site shall provide the following pages in each of the four languages, 21 per language and 84 in total: Home; Rooms and suites overview; one page for each of six room types (Family Suite, Executive Suite, Superior Twin Sea View, Superior Queen Sea View, Triple Deluxe Twin (pool view), Triple Deluxe Queen (pool view)); Dining; Pools and beach; Activities and facilities; Location; Offers; Gallery; FAQ; Contact Us; Privacy notice; Cookie notice; Terms; Accessibility statement; Newsletter unsubscribe. Each page shall have its own URL per language. Source: brief Scope In; Analyst (accessibility statement, unsubscribe page). Verify: T, I.

**FR-2 Rooms content.** The Rooms and suites page shall list all six room types, each as a card with name, one image, a short description and a link to its room page. Each room page shall show the room name, a description of at least 60 words, at least 3 images, a features list of at least 5 items and a Book now link (FR-11). Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T.

**FR-3 Dining content.** The Dining page shall present all ten venues named in the brief (Aqua Beach Restaurant, Oscar Restaurant, Italian Restaurant, Main Restaurant, French Cafe, Shisha Lounge, Lobby Bar, Lobby Lounge and Terrace, Twist Pool Bar, Splash Pool Bar), each with name, description of at least 25 words and one image, and each individually reachable by an anchor in the URL. Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T.

**FR-4 Pools, beach, activities and facilities content.** The Pools and beach page shall present four items (infinity pool, relaxing pool, kids' pool, private beach). The Activities and facilities page shall present five items (spa, gym, tennis court, kids' club and playground, entertainment). Each item shall have a name, a description of at least 25 words and one image. Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T.

**FR-5 Home content.** The Home page shall contain: the 3D hero (FR-27) with the resort name as page heading and a Book now link and a link to Contact Us; a resort overview section; a key facts section (qualitative facts only, see FR-18); a current offers summary showing the titles of the offers from the Offers page with a link to it; and links to Rooms, Dining, Pools and beach, Activities and facilities, Location and Gallery. Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T.

**FR-6 Location content.** The Location page shall show a static map image (no map tiles loaded from a third party at view time), a link labelled for opening the resort's position in Google Maps, and a list of at least 5 nearby attractions each with a name and a description of at least 15 words and no distances or travel times. Source: brief Scope In and Integrations (static map or link). Verify: T, I.

**FR-7 Offers content.** The Offers page shall list at least 3 offers, each with a title, a description of at least 25 words, validity wording in plain text and a Book now link. No offer shall show a price, a discount amount or a currency symbol. Source: brief Scope In; Analyst (no prices, see D4 and the consumer protection flag). Verify: T.

**FR-8 Gallery.** The Gallery page shall show at least 12 images in a grid. Activating an image shall open a lightbox showing the large image, its caption, previous and next controls, a close control and an image counter. The lightbox shall be operable with keyboard alone (arrow keys for previous and next, Escape to close), shall keep keyboard focus inside while open, and shall return focus to the activating image on close. Every image shall have alternative text in each language. Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T, M.

**FR-9 FAQ.** The FAQ page shall list at least 8 questions, each expanding to show its answer. Each question shall be a button operable with Enter and Space, shall expose its expanded or collapsed state to assistive technology, and its answer shall be present in the page without JavaScript (readable when JavaScript is off). Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T.

**FR-10 Global navigation and footer.** Every public page shall have: a header with the resort name linking to Home, links to Rooms and suites, Dining, Pools and beach, Activities and facilities, Offers, Gallery, Location and Contact Us, a Book now link and a language switcher; a menu that collapses behind a button below 960 CSS pixels wide and opens and closes with keyboard and touch; the current page marked with `aria-current="page"`; and a footer with the newsletter sign-up form (FR-22), the contact links and social links (FR-12), links to Privacy notice, Cookie notice, Terms and Accessibility statement, and the concept notice (FR-17). Source: brief Scope In; Analyst (accessibility statement link). Verify: T.

**FR-11 Book now links.** Every Book now link on the site shall use one destination URL held as a single admin setting (FR-39), shall open in a new browser tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, and shall have an accessible name that includes the words "opens in a new tab" in the page language. The site shall not collect, display or calculate availability, prices or payment details. Source: D4; brief Scope Out. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-15) for the destination URL.

**FR-12 Contact details and external links.** The site shall show, from admin settings (FR-39): a telephone number as a `tel:` link, a WhatsApp click-to-chat link of the form `https://wa.me/<number>`, an email address as a `mailto:` link, a postal address, a Google Maps link, and social links to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and YouTube. External links shall open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. A social or WhatsApp link with no value set shall not be rendered. The default values shall be synthetic placeholders. Source: brief Scope In and Integrations. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-16) for default values.

**FR-13 Error pages.** The site shall show a localised "page not found" page for any unknown URL (HTTP 404) and a localised "something went wrong" page for server errors (HTTP 500), each with the header, footer, concept notice, a link to Home and no technical detail (no stack trace, file path or software version). Source: Analyst. Verify: T.

### Legal pages and concept marking

**FR-14 Privacy notice content.** The Privacy notice shall state in each language: who the controller is and how to contact them; each purpose (answering enquiries, newsletter, admin accounts, security logging); the lawful basis for each purpose; the categories of data collected by each form; who receives the data; where it is stored and whether it leaves the country of the server; the retention period for each category (matching the configured values, FR-45); the individual's rights and how to use them; the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office; and that the site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It shall be linked from the footer of every page and, by a link in the consent text, from every form. Source: D1; brief Data and legal flags. Verify: I, T. Provisional (OQ-12) for the controller.

**FR-15 Cookie and storage notice.** The Cookie notice shall list every cookie and every browser storage item the site can set, with name, purpose, category (strictly necessary or not), lifetime and which pages set it, in each language. The list shall match what the site actually sets. Source: D1; brief Data and legal flags (PECR). Verify: T, I.

**FR-16 Terms, accessibility statement and about this concept.** The Terms page shall include a section "About this concept" saying that the site is an unofficial, speculative design concept, is not operated, authorised or endorsed by JAZ Hotels or Jaz Elite Casa del Mar Beach, uses placeholder images and text, and does not accept bookings. The Accessibility statement shall state the target (WCAG 2.2 level AA), the date it was last reviewed, known limitations (empty if none) and a route to report a problem. Source: D2; Analyst. Verify: I, T.

**FR-17 Concept marking.** Every page of the public site, every error page and every admin portal screen shall show a concept notice that is visible without scrolling on load, cannot be dismissed or hidden by the visitor, is in the page language, occupies no more than 48 CSS pixels of height on screens narrower than 600 pixels, never covers page content or keyboard focus, and states that the site is an unofficial concept not affiliated with JAZ Hotels. The browser title of every page shall end with the word "Concept" (translated). The notice wording shall be editable per language in the admin portal (FR-39) but shall never be empty. Source: D2. Verify: T, M. Provisional (OQ-5) for editability, (OQ-17) for naming.

**FR-18 Placeholder content rules.** All images, 3D models and text shall be original, generated, or licensed royalty free for commercial use, and none shall be copied from jazhotels.com. Every image and font shall have an entry in a licence register recording its source, licence and date. No page shall show a price, a currency amount, a star rating, an award, a certification, a review quote or a review score, or a logo or badge of JAZ, Tripadvisor or any other third party. Numeric facts about the resort in text shall be limited to the counts of items named in this document. The site shall not hotlink or request any asset from jazhotels.com. Source: D2; brief Content and brand. Verify: T, I. Provisional (OQ-17, OQ-19).

### Forms

**FR-19 Contact form fields and validation.** The Contact Us page shall provide a form with: name (required, 2 to 100 characters), email address (required, valid address format, at most 254 characters), telephone (optional, 7 to 20 characters from digits, spaces, plus, brackets and hyphens), message (required, 10 to 2000 characters), and a consent checkbox, unticked by default and required, next to text stating what the data is used for and linking to the Privacy notice. The form shall also contain a hidden spam trap field (FR-20). Validation shall run in the browser and again on the server, with identical rules. Error messages shall be in the page language, be shown next to each field and in a summary at the top of the form, and name the field and the problem. No other personal data shall be requested. Source: D1; brief Integrations. Verify: T.

**FR-20 Contact form submission.** On a valid submission the server shall store the enquiry (name, email, telephone if given, message, language of the page, time received, consent wording version and time of consent), assign a reference of the form `ENQ-` followed by 6 characters from A to Z and 2 to 9, and show a confirmation page in the page language that states the reference and that the enquiry was received. A submission shall be rejected without storing it when the spam trap field is filled in or when it is received less than 2 seconds after the form was displayed; the rejection shall not reveal which test failed. The form shall work with JavaScript turned off (a normal form post with a redirect). When the server cannot store the enquiry the visitor shall see an error message, the typed values shall be kept in the form, and no confirmation shall be shown. Source: D1; brief Integrations. Verify: T.

**FR-21 Enquiry email notification (optional).** The site shall be able to send one email to a configured staff address for each new enquiry, using the SMTP host and credentials given in server configuration. The feature shall be off unless configured. The email shall contain the reference, the date and a link to the enquiry in the admin portal, and shall not contain the message text, the visitor's name, email address or telephone. A failure to send shall not prevent the enquiry being stored or the confirmation being shown. Source: brief open question 2. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-2).

**FR-22 Newsletter sign-up.** The footer of every public page shall contain a sign-up form with an email address field (valid format, at most 254 characters) and a consent checkbox, unticked by default and required, with text stating that the address will be used to send the resort's newsletter, that the person can unsubscribe at any time, and a link to the Privacy notice. A valid submission shall store the email address, the page language, the time, the consent wording version, and the status "active", and shall show a confirmation message in the page language. Submitting an address that is already subscribed shall show the same confirmation message and shall not create a second record. The form shall work with JavaScript turned off. Source: D1; brief Scope In. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-11) for opt-in method.

**FR-23 Newsletter unsubscribe.** The Newsletter unsubscribe page shall let anyone enter an email address and confirm, after which any active subscriber record for that address shall be set to "unsubscribed" and no longer included in the default export (FR-43). The page shall show the same result message whether or not the address was subscribed. The page shall work with JavaScript turned off. Source: D1; Analyst. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-11).

### Languages

**FR-24 Four languages.** Every public page in the page inventory, every form label, button, error message, confirmation message, alternative text and legal text shall be available in English, German, Italian and Russian. Each language shall have its own URL path. Every page shall have a language switcher listing all four languages by their own names (English, Deutsch, Italiano, Русский), which takes the visitor to the same page in the chosen language (or the language's Home if that page does not exist) and marks the current language. The `lang` attribute of the page shall be the page language. Source: D5. Verify: T.

**FR-25 Default language and detection.** A request to the site root shall redirect to a language page. The target language shall be chosen from the browser's `Accept-Language` header: the first listed language among en, de, it and ru; English if none matches. The choice shall be made per request and shall not be stored in a cookie. A visitor who picks a language using the switcher stays in that language by following links, because the language is in the URL. Source: brief open question 3. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-3).

**FR-26 Missing translations.** When a text field has no value in the page language, the public site shall show the English value for that field and shall set `lang="en"` on the element containing it. A page shall never be shown with an empty heading, empty paragraph or empty button because of a missing translation. Source: D5. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-7).

### Motion and 3D

**FR-27 Immersive 3D hero.** The Home page shall open with a real-time 3D hero scene (a Red Sea seascape or resort scene, built by the agency) that renders in a canvas behind the hero text. The scene shall react to the visitor: pointer movement on devices with a mouse, device tilt or touch drag on phones (without blocking vertical page scroll). The hero heading, text and links shall be real page text, not drawn in the canvas. Until the scene has rendered its first frame, and in fallback mode, a static poster image of the scene shall be shown in the same place. Source: brief Motion appetite ("Immersive") and Goal. Verify: T, M.

**FR-28 Landing scenes.** Each of the section landing pages (Rooms and suites, Dining, Pools and beach, Activities and facilities, Location) shall have one distinct motion scene, either a real-time 3D scene or a scroll-driven scene, near the top of the page, each with its own static fallback image. Source: brief Goal and Motion appetite. Verify: T, M. Provisional (OQ-9).

**FR-29 Loader and page transitions.** The site shall show: (a) a branded loader screen on first visit to the site in a browser tab, shown only until the hero assets are ready; (b) a transition screen when moving between pages in the same tab, containing the resort name and a progress or motion indicator. Both shall be removed automatically as soon as the next page is ready and no later than 8 seconds after they start, after which the page shall be shown regardless of unfinished 3D loading. They shall announce "Loading" to assistive technology, shall move focus to the new page's main heading when the page is shown, shall update the browser title and address, and shall leave the browser back and forward buttons working. Without JavaScript, links shall still navigate normally with no transition. Source: brief Goal ("loader pages between pages"). Verify: T, M.

**FR-30 Motion controls.** Every page that has autonomous motion longer than 5 seconds (3D hero, landing scenes, looping effects) shall have a visible control, reachable by keyboard, that pauses all such motion on the page and a way to resume it. Pausing shall freeze 3D scenes on their current frame, stop page transitions from animating (content swaps with no motion) and stop CSS and scroll-driven effects. The choice shall be kept only for the browser tab session. Source: Analyst (WCAG 2.2.2); brief Motion appetite. Verify: T, M.

**FR-31 3D asset authoring and delivery.** All 3D models shall be created by the agency (no third-party models), exported as glTF binary (.glb) files with geometry compression, and delivered with their editable source files in the release package together with a statement of authorship and licence. Each scene shall load only its own assets. Source: brief Content and brand ("for 3D models create here"). Verify: I.

### Admin portal

**FR-32 Admin sign in and sign out.** The admin portal shall be at its own path, shall require sign in with email address and password for every screen and every admin data request, and shall provide sign out that ends the session on the server. Failed sign in shall show one generic message that does not say whether the email address or the password was wrong. There shall be no guest or demonstration account and no default password. The admin portal interface shall be in English. Source: D3. Verify: T.

**FR-33 Two-step verification.** Every admin account shall be required to enrol a time-based one-time password authenticator (the open TOTP method, usable with free authenticator apps) at first sign in, and to enter a current code at each sign in. Ten single-use recovery codes shall be issued at enrolment, shown once, and stored only as hashes. An Administrator shall be able to reset another account's enrolment. Source: Analyst (security); brief Scope In (staff login). Verify: T.

**FR-34 Admin accounts.** An Administrator shall be able to create, disable, enable and delete admin accounts and set an initial password that must be changed at first sign in. Every user shall be able to change their own password. A password reset shall be possible without email, by an Administrator setting a new temporary password or by a server-side command documented in the hosting guide; reset by email link shall exist only if email sending is configured (OQ-2). Roles: Administrator (all functions) and Editor (content, media and settings editing, FR-35 to FR-41; no access to enquiries, subscribers, accounts, audit log or data subject tools). The last active Administrator shall not be deletable or disableable. Source: D3; brief Scope In. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-1).

**FR-35 Edit all content.** The admin portal shall let a signed-in user edit, in every language, every piece of text shown on the public site: page headings and titles, body text, button and link labels, navigation labels, footer text, form labels, help text, validation and confirmation messages, the browser title and meta description of each page, the legal pages, the concept notice wording, image alternative text and captions. Body text shall support paragraphs, bold, italic, links and bulleted lists, and no other markup. Source: D3. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-4) for what "everything" includes.

**FR-36 Manage list items.** The admin portal shall let a signed-in user add, edit, delete and reorder the items in these lists: rooms (within the six-page structure), dining venues, pools and beach items, activities and facilities items, offers, gallery images, FAQ questions, nearby attractions, room features. The public site shall show items in the saved order. Deleting an item shall require a confirmation step naming the item. The minimum counts in FR-2 to FR-9 shall not be enforced as blockers in the admin portal, but the admin screen shall warn when a list falls below its minimum. Source: D3. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-4).

**FR-37 Translation editing and completeness.** For every editable text field the admin screen shall show the four language values together, and for every page and list item a completeness indicator of the form "3 of 4 languages complete". A field counts as complete when it has non-empty text. The portal shall provide one screen listing all fields missing in any language, filterable by language. Source: D3; D5. Verify: T.

**FR-38 Publishing and change visibility.** Saved changes shall reach the public site without rebuilding or restarting the software. Provisional (OQ-6): each page has a draft and a published version; saving writes the draft; a Preview action shows the draft exactly as it would appear publicly, with the concept notice and an extra "Preview" label; a Publish action, after a confirmation step, makes the draft public within 60 seconds; the portal keeps the last 10 published versions of each page and lets the user restore one as a new draft; leaving an edit screen with unsaved changes shows a warning. Source: D3. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-6).

**FR-39 Site settings.** The admin portal shall provide a settings screen for: the Book now destination URL (must start with `https://`), telephone number, WhatsApp number, email address, postal address, Google Maps link, the five social links, and the concept notice wording per language. Invalid values (for example a URL not starting with `https://`, or a telephone number with letters) shall be rejected with a message. There shall be no setting that removes the concept notice or changes the noindex rules (NFR-27). Source: D3; brief Integrations. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-5).

**FR-40 Media library.** The admin portal shall let a signed-in user upload photos (individually or up to 20 at once), see them in a library with thumbnail, name, size, upload date and where each is used, enter alternative text and a caption for each of the four languages, replace an image file while keeping its uses, and delete an image. Deleting an image used on a public page shall be refused with a list of the pages that use it. A photo shall not be usable on a public page until it has alternative text in the default language (alt text in other languages follows FR-26). Source: D3. Verify: T.

**FR-41 Image processing.** For every uploaded photo the server shall create resized copies at 480, 768, 1280 and 1920 pixels wide (not larger than the original) in AVIF and WebP, shall keep the original file outside the public area, shall apply the camera orientation and shall remove all embedded metadata (including location) from every copy served to the public. Public pages shall serve these copies with `srcset` and `sizes`, explicit width and height, and `loading="lazy"` for images outside the first screen. Source: D3; Analyst (privacy, performance). Verify: T, I.

**FR-42 Enquiries.** An Administrator shall be able to see all enquiries newest first, in pages of 25, open one to read it in full, mark it Handled or Unhandled, filter by status and search by reference, name or email address, and delete one after confirmation. New enquiries shall be shown as unread until opened, with the count of unread enquiries on the portal's main screen. Source: D3. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-1) for role.

**FR-43 Subscribers and export.** An Administrator shall be able to see all subscribers with email address, language, status, date and consent wording version, search by address, remove one subscriber after confirmation, and export subscribers as a CSV file. The export shall: contain only active subscribers unless the user ticks "include unsubscribed"; be UTF-8 with a byte order mark; have the columns `email`, `language`, `status`, `subscribed_at`, `consent_version`; escape any cell starting with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` so spreadsheets do not run it as a formula; and be recorded in the audit log (FR-46) with the user, time and number of rows. Source: D3. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-1) for role.

**FR-44 Data subject request tools.** An Administrator shall be able to enter an email address and see every record held for it (enquiries, subscriber record, admin account, audit log entries that name it), download those records as a JSON file, and delete them (with confirmation, except audit log entries, which are anonymised, not deleted). Source: D1; NFR-16. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-1) for role.

**FR-45 Retention enforcement.** The site shall run a purge job at least once every 24 hours that permanently deletes or anonymises personal data older than its configured retention period, as set out in the Retention schedule below, and shall record in the audit log each run and the number of records removed per category. Retention periods shall be configuration values changeable without changing code. Source: D1. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-10) for the periods.

**FR-46 Audit log.** The site shall record, with time, acting user (or "anonymous" or "system") and result: every admin sign in attempt, success and failure, sign out, session timeout, account creation, change, disabling and deletion, password change or reset, enrolment or reset of two-step verification, publish and restore, settings change, media upload and delete, enquiry read and delete, subscriber removal, subscriber export, data subject tool use and each purge run. An Administrator shall be able to view and filter the log by date, user and event type. The log shall not contain passwords, one-time codes, session identifiers, enquiry text or subscriber email addresses. Source: Analyst (security, accountability). Verify: T.

### Delivery

**FR-47 Local review package.** The release shall be a package that a reviewer can install and run on one computer using documented commands, that binds to the local machine address only by default, that needs no account with any external service, and that on first run asks the person to create the first Administrator (no default credentials). The package shall include the placeholder content in all four languages, so a reviewer sees a complete site on first run. Source: D2 (local review only); brief Constraints. Verify: T, M.

**FR-48 Backup and restore.** The package shall include a documented command that writes all site data (content, media, enquiries, subscribers, accounts, settings) to a single archive file, and a documented command that restores such an archive into an empty install. After a restore, the public site and the admin portal shall show the same content and records as before the backup. The archive shall be marked as containing personal data in its file name or accompanying text. Source: Analyst (operability, no paid backup service). Verify: T.

## Non-functional requirements

### Accessibility

**NFR-1 WCAG 2.2 level AA.** All public pages (all 84 URLs in the page inventory), the error pages, all form states (error, confirmation) and all admin portal screens shall meet every WCAG 2.2 level A and AA success criterion, in 3D mode and in fallback mode. Evidence shall be an automated scan with axe-core (version recorded) giving zero violations tagged `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21aa` or `wcag22aa` on every page and state, plus the manual checks in NFR-2 to NFR-5. Lighthouse accessibility score shall be at least 95 on every public page. Source: brief Data and legal flags; agency standard. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-2 Keyboard and focus.** Every function on the public site and in the admin portal shall be usable with a keyboard alone. Tab order shall follow reading order. A visible focus indicator of at least 2 CSS pixels with 3 to 1 contrast against adjacent colours shall show on every focusable element, and the focused element shall never be fully hidden by the concept notice, the sticky header or any consent control (WCAG 2.4.11). A "Skip to main content" link shall be the first focusable element on every page. No keyboard trap shall exist, including inside 3D scenes, the lightbox (which closes with Escape) and the menu. Pointer targets shall be at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels (WCAG 2.5.8). Every drag interaction shall have a non-drag alternative (WCAG 2.5.7). Source: WCAG 2.2. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-3 Screen reader support.** Pages shall use landmarks (header, nav, main, footer), exactly one `h1` per page, a heading order with no skipped levels, a language attribute on the page and on any text in another language, accessible names for all controls, status messages announced without moving focus (WCAG 4.1.3), and form errors tied to their fields. A 3D canvas shall be hidden from assistive technology (`aria-hidden="true"`), with the hero's meaning carried by real page text; informative images shall have alternative text and decorative images an empty `alt`. The journeys Home to Rooms to a room page, submit the contact form, subscribe to the newsletter, and (admin) sign in, edit a text, upload a photo shall be completed with NVDA on Windows with Firefox, and with VoiceOver on iOS with Safari, without sight. Source: WCAG 2.2; D2. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-4 Visual presentation.** Text contrast shall be at least 4.5 to 1 (3 to 1 for text of 24 CSS pixels or larger, or 19 pixels bold) and contrast of interface components and graphics at least 3 to 1. Text drawn over images, video or 3D shall meet this over every frame of a scene and on the poster image, by means of a solid or gradient backing, checked at the worst frame. Pages shall reflow without horizontal scrolling or loss of content at 320 CSS pixels wide, work at 200 percent zoom, and tolerate WCAG text spacing overrides without content clipping. Meaning shall not rely on colour alone. Body text shall be at least 16 CSS pixels. Source: WCAG 2.2. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-5 Authentication accessibility and time.** The admin sign in shall allow pasting into every field, allow password managers, and set `autocomplete` values (`username`, `current-password`, `one-time-code`), and shall not require solving a puzzle or transcribing characters (WCAG 3.3.8). Information the user has already given in the same process shall not be asked for again (WCAG 3.3.7). Any session timeout shall warn at least 2 minutes before it happens and allow extension (WCAG 2.2.1). Source: WCAG 2.2. Verify: T, M.

### Performance

**NFR-6 Core Web Vitals budgets.** On R1 or L1, cold cache, for each public page in each language, in 3D mode: Largest Contentful Paint at most 2.5 s; Cumulative Layout Shift at most 0.1; Total Blocking Time at most 300 ms; First Contentful Paint at most 1.8 s; and Interaction to Next Paint at most 200 ms measured over a scripted set of interactions (open menu, switch language, open and close a lightbox image, expand a FAQ item, type in a form field). Server response time for page HTML shall be at most 200 ms when the package runs on the machine used for review. The Lighthouse mobile Performance score shall be at least 80 in 3D mode and at least 90 in fallback mode, and the Best Practices score at least 95. (The Lighthouse SEO category is excluded because the concept is deliberately noindex.) Source: brief Constraints (mid range phones); agency budgets. Verify: A.

**NFR-7 Weight budgets.** Measured as compressed bytes transferred on first load of a page with a cold cache and no scrolling: all content needed before any 3D loads (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts and the largest above-the-fold image) at most 600 KB; JavaScript excluding the 3D chunk at most 200 KB; CSS at most 60 KB; web fonts at most 120 KB in total and at most 2 font families, with only the character subsets the page language needs (the Cyrillic file shall not be requested on en, de or it pages); the 3D code chunk (the 3D library and scene code) at most 350 KB; 3D assets (models and textures) for the Home hero at most 3 MB and for each other scene at most 2 MB; hero poster image at most 150 KB; every other delivered image file at most 250 KB. 3D code and assets shall be requested only after the page's text and poster have rendered. Source: brief Constraints; agency budgets. Verify: A, T.

**NFR-8 3D runtime budget.** On R1, over a scripted 20 second run of each scene including interaction: median frame rate at least 50 frames per second and the slowest 1 percent of frames no slower than 30 frames per second; no more than 150,000 triangles and no more than 100 draw calls in any frame; render resolution capped at a device pixel ratio of 1.5 on touch devices and 2 on others; first rendered frame within 4 s of navigation start on L1. If the median frame time over any 2 second window exceeds 22 ms, the scene shall reduce its quality by one step (render scale, effects, shadows), up to three steps; if after the lowest step the median frame time still exceeds 33 ms for 3 consecutive seconds, the page shall switch to fallback mode. Rendering shall stop within 1 second when the browser tab is hidden or the scene is off screen, and resume when it returns. Only one WebGL context shall exist at a time, and after 20 navigations between pages the JavaScript heap, measured after a forced garbage collection, shall have grown by at most 25 MB from its size after first load. Source: brief Motion appetite; agency budgets. Verify: A, T.

**NFR-9 Transition and loader timing.** On R1 or L1 a page transition (exit plus entry) shall take at most 800 ms when the next page is ready, shall block user input for no more than 100 ms in total, and shall not by itself increase the next page's Largest Contentful Paint by more than 200 ms. The initial loader shall appear only if the page is not ready within 300 ms, and shall never be visible for more than 8 seconds. Source: brief Goal; FR-29. Verify: A, T.

**NFR-10 Server and admin performance.** On a server of 1 CPU and 1 GB memory (OQ-14): public page HTML responds within 200 ms at the 95th percentile with 25 simultaneous visitors; admin read requests respond within 500 ms at the 95th percentile; the enquiries list shows its first page within 1 s when 5,000 enquiries exist; a 10 MB photo upload is processed (FR-41) within 30 s; a CSV export of 10,000 subscribers completes within 10 s. Source: Analyst; D6 (no running cost, so small servers). Verify: A.

### Reduced motion, fallback and no-JavaScript

**NFR-11 Reduced motion.** When the visitor's system asks for reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`), the site shall: not start any 3D scene (the poster image is shown instead), not start any parallax, scroll-driven animation, auto-playing animation or looping effect, replace page transitions and the loader with an instant swap or an opacity change of at most 150 ms, and not zoom, slide or spin any element. All content, links and functions shall remain available. The motion controls (FR-30) shall still be present where motion exists. Source: brief Motion appetite; WCAG 2.3.3. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-12 Non-WebGL and low-capability fallback.** The site shall switch to fallback mode, showing for each scene its static poster image (at most 300 KB per image, with no layout shift against 3D mode) and no 3D code or 3D asset download, when any of these is true: the browser has no WebGL 2 support; creating the WebGL context fails; the context is lost and not restored within 3 seconds; the visitor asked for reduced motion (NFR-11); the connection reports data saving (`Save-Data`) or an effective type of 2g or slow-2g; the visitor has paused motion (FR-30); the adaptive rule in NFR-8 has triggered. In fallback mode every text, link, form and function of the page shall be present and behave exactly as in 3D mode, and the page shall pass NFR-1 and the fallback performance scores in NFR-6. Source: brief Motion appetite; design standard. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-13 Progressive enhancement.** With JavaScript turned off, every public page shall display all its text and images, navigation and language switching shall work, the contact form, newsletter sign-up and unsubscribe shall work by normal form submission, the FAQ answers shall be readable, and the concept notice shall be shown. Gallery lightbox, 3D, loader and transitions are the only functions allowed to be absent. The admin portal may require JavaScript. Source: Analyst; brief Constraints (graceful fallback). Verify: T.

### Privacy

**NFR-14 Data protection by design.** The site shall collect only the data fields listed in FR-19, FR-20, FR-22, FR-23, FR-32 to FR-34 and FR-46 and nothing else; shall use the data only for the purposes stated in the Privacy notice; shall record for every enquiry and subscriber the wording version of the consent text they agreed to and the time; shall not pre-tick any consent box; shall not make any service conditional on newsletter consent; shall not ask for or infer anyone's age and shall not be directed at children; and shall not send personal data to any third party. Application logs shall not contain enquiry text, email addresses, passwords or one-time codes. Source: D1; brief Data and legal flags. Verify: T, I. Provisional (OQ-12) for lawful basis wording.

**NFR-15 Cookies, storage and third-party requests (PECR).** The public site shall set no cookie and write nothing to local or session storage before or without the visitor's explicit opt-in, except for items that are strictly necessary or that the visitor explicitly asked for (the pause-motion choice in session storage, FR-30, and the admin session cookie, which is set only on the admin portal). No analytics, advertising, social media widget, embedded video, embedded map or tag manager shall be present. Fonts, scripts, images and all other assets shall be served from the site's own origin: a page load shall make zero requests to any other host. If a non-essential cookie or storage item is ever added, a consent control shall first be added that: shows before the item is set, offers Accept and Reject with equal prominence, is off by default, lets the visitor change or withdraw the choice at any time from a link in the footer, and records nothing until accepted. Source: D1; brief Data and legal flags (PECR); D6. Verify: T, I.

**NFR-16 Individual rights.** The site shall support, through FR-44, finding, exporting, correcting (by deleting and re-entering) and deleting all personal data held for an email address. A search shall take at most 5 s. The hosting guide and Privacy notice shall state that requests are answered within one calendar month. Source: D1. Verify: T, I.

**NFR-17 Retention.** Personal data shall be kept no longer than the periods in the Retention schedule below. Backup archives created by FR-48 shall be kept no longer than 35 days by default and the backup command shall delete older archives it created. Source: D1. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-10).

**NFR-18 Storage location and protection.** All personal data shall be stored only in the site's own storage on the server running the site. Passwords shall be stored only as salted hashes (NFR-19). Pages that collect personal data and the whole admin portal shall be served over HTTPS whenever the site is reachable from another machine; the package shall refuse to start on a non-local address without HTTPS configured. Uploaded originals, backup archives and the data store shall not be reachable by any public URL. Source: D1; D6. Verify: T, I.

### Security

**NFR-19 Admin authentication strength.** Passwords shall be at least 12 characters, with no maximum below 128 characters and no composition rules, shall be rejected if they appear in a bundled list of at least 10,000 common passwords or contain the account's email address, and shall be stored with Argon2id (memory at least 19 MiB, at least 2 iterations, 1 degree of parallelism) or, if Argon2id is unavailable on the server, bcrypt with cost at least 12. After 5 failed sign in attempts for one account within 15 minutes the account shall be locked for 15 minutes (the lock applies to the account whatever the source address, and the response shall not reveal whether an account exists). Response time for unknown and known accounts shall not differ by more than 100 ms at the median. Source: D3; Analyst. Verify: T, I.

**NFR-20 Sessions.** The admin session shall use a random identifier of at least 128 bits, held in a cookie marked `HttpOnly` and `SameSite=Strict` and, whenever the page is served over HTTPS, `Secure`; shall be replaced at sign in; shall end after 30 minutes without activity and 8 hours after sign in; shall end on sign out and on password change; and no admin response shall be cacheable (`Cache-Control: no-store`). Source: D3; Analyst. Verify: T.

**NFR-21 Rate limiting.** The server shall limit requests per client address and return HTTP 429 with a `Retry-After` header when exceeded: admin sign in at most 20 attempts per 15 minutes; contact form at most 5 submissions per hour; newsletter sign-up at most 5 per hour; newsletter unsubscribe at most 10 per hour; admin write requests at most 300 per minute per session; media uploads at most 30 per hour per user. Counters shall be kept no longer than 24 hours. Source: Analyst (security); D1. Verify: T.

**NFR-22 Request forgery protection.** Every request that changes state (all public forms, sign out, and every admin action) shall be accepted only with a valid anti-forgery token tied to the session or form, and only when the `Origin` or `Referer` header, if present, matches the site's own origin. State-changing actions shall never use the HTTP GET method. A request lacking the token shall be refused with HTTP 403 and shall change nothing. Source: Analyst (security). Verify: T.

**NFR-23 Upload validation.** The server shall accept an upload only from a signed-in user with permission, only if its content (not its file name or declared type) is a JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF image, its size is at most 10 MB, its dimensions are at most 40 megapixels, and the batch has at most 20 files. The server shall refuse every other type, including SVG, GIF, PDF, HTML and archives, and files whose extension disagrees with their content. Stored file names shall be random, the upload folder shall not run scripts, files shall be served with the correct media type and `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, and path characters in uploaded names shall never reach the file system. Total media storage shall have a configurable limit (default 2 GB) with a warning in the admin portal at 80 percent and refusal at 100 percent. Source: D3; Analyst. Verify: T.

**NFR-24 Injection and output safety.** All input shall be validated on the server by type, length and format; database access shall use parameterised queries only; all output shall be encoded for its context; formatted body text (FR-35) shall be passed through an allow-list sanitiser that keeps only paragraphs, bold, italic, links (with `https:`, `http:`, `mailto:` and `tel:` schemes only) and lists; and the pages shall carry these headers: `Content-Security-Policy` with `default-src 'self'`, no `unsafe-inline` and no `unsafe-eval` in `script-src` (`wasm-unsafe-eval` is allowed only if a WebAssembly decoder is used for 3D assets), `object-src 'none'`, `base-uri 'self'`, `form-action 'self'` and `frame-ancestors 'none'`; `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`; `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`; a `Permissions-Policy` that disables camera, microphone, geolocation and payment; and, over HTTPS, `Strict-Transport-Security` with a `max-age` of at least 15,552,000 seconds. No CSP violation shall be logged by the browser during the end-to-end tests. Source: Analyst (security). Verify: T, I.

**NFR-25 Authorisation.** Every admin route and every admin data request shall be refused with HTTP 401 (not signed in) or 403 (not permitted) unless the signed-in user holds the permission; access shall be denied by default for routes with no rule; a user shall not be able to read or change a record by guessing its identifier (every record request is checked for permission, not only for sign-in); and the roles in FR-34 shall be enforced on the server, not only hidden in the interface. Source: D3; Analyst. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-1).

**NFR-26 Secrets, errors and dependencies.** No secret, key, password or personal data shall be committed to any repository or included in the release package other than synthetic examples; secrets shall be read from server configuration; error responses shall not expose stack traces, file paths, query text or software versions; a dependency audit (the package manager's built-in audit or equivalent) shall report no known vulnerability of severity high or critical in anything shipped, at the time of release; dependencies shall be pinned by a lock file; and the configuration shall be secure by default (debug off, local address only). Source: Analyst; repo rules. Verify: T, I.

### Search engines, languages, browsers

**NFR-27 Noindex everywhere.** Every HTTP response that carries a page (public pages in all languages, error pages, form result pages, admin portal screens) shall carry both the header `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet` and the meta tag `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet">`. Image and file responses shall carry the header. The site shall serve no `sitemap.xml`, no canonical link and no alternate-language link pointing to the real JAZ domain, and no structured data (JSON-LD, microdata) describing the resort as a business or hotel. No admin setting shall change these rules. Source: D2. Verify: T.

**NFR-28 Internationalisation.** All user-visible text shall come from language resource files or the content store, never from code, in all four languages; at release 100 percent of public strings shall have a value in all four languages. Pages shall be encoded in UTF-8. Fonts shall contain every glyph used in the four languages, including the German letters ä ö ü ß, the Italian accented letters à è é ì ò ù and the Russian Cyrillic alphabet. Layouts shall show without clipped, overlapping or overflowing text when every string is 40 percent longer than its English version, at widths from 320 to 1920 CSS pixels. Form validation messages, confirmation messages and error pages shall be localised. Sorting and search in the admin portal shall treat Cyrillic and accented letters correctly. Source: D5. Verify: T, I.

**NFR-29 Browser, device and viewport support.** The public site shall work fully, in 3D mode where WebGL 2 is available and in fallback mode otherwise, in: the latest 2 stable major versions of Chrome, Edge and Firefox (Windows, macOS; Chrome also on Android); Safari 16.4 and later on macOS, iOS and iPadOS; and the latest 2 stable versions of Samsung Internet. It shall work at every viewport width from 320 to 2560 CSS pixels in portrait and landscape. The admin portal shall work in the desktop and tablet versions of the same browsers at widths from 768 CSS pixels. In other browsers the site shall still show its text and links. Source: brief Constraints (agency default). Verify: T, M.

### Cost, operations, brand

**NFR-30 Brand.** The design shall use brand elements recorded by the designer from the current site (theme colour #453536, the tone of short upbeat labels, and any fonts and palette found), together with a written list of what was extracted and what was enhanced for the resort and sector; no logo artwork, badge or image shall be copied (FR-18). The brand colours and type scale shall be defined once as design tokens and every colour pairing in use shall meet NFR-4. Source: D7. Verify: I.

**NFR-31 Zero running cost.** Operating the site shall need no paid third-party service, no subscription, no account with a third-party provider and no per-use fee. All software shipped or required at run time shall be licensed under a licence on the project's allow-list (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, 0BSD, CC0-1.0, MPL-2.0, SIL OFL-1.1 for fonts), and any other licence needs written approval recorded in the licence register. The site shall store content, media, enquiries and subscribers in its own storage, send email only through the client's own SMTP host, and make no request to any external host while running (apart from links a visitor follows). The full test suite of the visitor and admin journeys shall pass when the machine's outgoing network is blocked, except to the configured SMTP host. The site shall run in 512 MB of memory at rest and in 1 GB under the load in NFR-10, on one CPU. Source: D6; brief Constraints. Verify: T, I, A. Provisional (OQ-14) for the server.

**NFR-32 Operability.** All settings (listening address and port, data folder, SMTP host and credentials, retention periods, session limits, upload limits) shall be read from one documented configuration file or environment variables, with an example file containing no secrets. The package shall provide a health check that returns HTTP 200 only when the data store is readable and writable. The documented install and first run shall take no more than 10 commands. Source: Analyst; brief Constraints (hosting by the client, hosting guide before release). Verify: T, I.

**NFR-33 Data integrity.** An enquiry, subscriber record or content change that the site has confirmed to the user shall survive an abrupt stop of the software (power loss, process kill) immediately afterwards. Content saves shall be all-or-nothing: an interrupted save shall leave the previous content intact. Two staff saving the same page shall not silently overwrite each other: the second save shall be refused with a message and the chance to review the other change. Source: Analyst. Verify: T.

| Data | Proposed default retention | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
