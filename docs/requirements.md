## How to read this document

- IDs: functional requirements are FR-n, non-functional requirements are NFR-n, open questions are OQ-n, legal register rows are LR-n, security gaps are G-n, gate findings are M-n and L-n. IDs are permanent: a withdrawn requirement keeps its number and is marked withdrawn.
- "Shall" marks a requirement. Every requirement is written so a person or a test can decide pass or fail without interpretation.
- Source: where the requirement comes from (a brief section, one of Steven's decisions numbered D1 to D7 in the brief's "Decisions from Steven", or "Analyst" where the analyst added it as standard practice; analyst additions are listed in `docs/assumptions.md` so Steven can reject them).
- Verify: T is an automated test, M is a manual test or demonstration by a person, I is an inspection of code, configuration or a document, A is a measurement or analysis. QA adds the actual tests later; this field only says what kind of evidence is expected.
- Provisional (OQ-n): the requirement depends on a question listed under "Open after T1". After T1 only OQ-12, OQ-20 and OQ-21 are used.

## Glossary and fixed definitions

- **Four languages**: English (en), German (de), Italian (it) and Russian (ru).
- **Page inventory**: the 21 page types per language listed in FR-1, 84 URLs in total.
- **Public site**: the pages visitors see. **Admin portal**: the staff-only area (FR-32 onwards).
- **Enquiry**: one submission of the Contact Us form. **Subscriber**: one newsletter sign-up record.
- **Concept notice**: the persistent notice defined in FR-17.
- **Reference phone R1**: a mid range Android phone of the 2022 to 2024 generation: Snapdragon 6 or 7 series, Exynos 1380 or Dimensity 7000 series class system on chip, 6 GB memory, 1080 by 2400 pixel display, current stable Chrome. The named example is the Samsung Galaxy A54 5G. **Reference phone R2**: iPhone 12 or later, Safari on iOS 16.4 or later.
- **Lab profile L1**: Chrome stable with the Lighthouse mobile preset: simulated slow 4G (1.6 Mbit/s down, 150 ms round trip), 4 times CPU slowdown, cold cache. Budgets marked "R1 or L1" are measured on R1 when the device is available and always on L1; if the two disagree the worse result counts.
- **3D mode**: the page is showing the real-time WebGL scenes. **Fallback mode**: the page is showing the static, non-WebGL presentation defined in NFR-12.
- **Mid range phone class**: R1 and R2 as defined above.
- **Synthetic data**: invented data that cannot identify a real person, for example `visitor.one@example.com`, `Test Visitor`, `+44 20 7946 0000`.
- **Administrator**: any signed-in staff account. This release has one role (OQ-1), so every Administrator can use every admin function.
- **Lab profile L2** (used only when R1 is not available): Chrome stable on a desktop or laptop with hardware graphics acceleration on, 6 times CPU slowdown, device pixel ratio 2.6, viewport 412 by 915 CSS pixels. Any result taken on L2 is labelled "emulated" in the test report, and the report lists confirmation on R1 as an open item for T3.
- **Fixed concept sentence**: the sentence held in the language resource files (not in the content store, so not editable in the admin portal) that starts the concept notice (FR-17). English: "Unofficial design concept. Not affiliated with or endorsed by JAZ Hotels." German: "Inoffizielles Designkonzept. Nicht mit JAZ Hotels verbunden und nicht von JAZ Hotels unterstützt." Italian: "Concept di design non ufficiale. Non affiliato né approvato da JAZ Hotels." Russian: "Неофициальная дизайн-концепция. Не связана с JAZ Hotels и не одобрена ими." The German, Italian and Russian wording is machine assisted and unreviewed (OQ-8).
- **Title suffix**: the word that ends every browser title (FR-17), added by the system and not editable: English "Concept", German "Konzept", Italian "Concept", Russian "Концепт", separated from the page title by a space, a vertical bar and a space.
- **Forbidden claims list** (FR-7, FR-18, AC-21.3): the following terms, matched as whole words, ignoring case, in the visible text of every public page. English: award, awards, award-winning, certified, certification, Travellers' Choice, Travelers' Choice, Green Star, Go Green, eco-label, star rating, guest review, review score, best, largest, biggest, finest, number one, only today, limited time, last chance, last rooms, hurry, countdown, expires. German: Auszeichnung, ausgezeichnet, zertifiziert, Zertifizierung, Green Star, Sterne-Bewertung, Gästebewertung, Bewertungspunktzahl, beste, bester, bestes, größte, größter, Nummer eins, nur heute, nur für kurze Zeit, letzte Chance, letzte Zimmer, beeilen. Italian: premio, premiato, certificato, certificazione, Green Star, classificazione a stelle, recensione degli ospiti, punteggio delle recensioni, migliore, più grande, numero uno, solo oggi, tempo limitato, ultima occasione, ultime camere, affrettati. Russian: награда, награждён, сертифицирован, сертификат, Green Star, рейтинг звёзд, отзывы гостей, оценка гостей, лучший, лучшая, лучшее, крупнейший, самый большой, номер один, только сегодня, ограниченное время, последний шанс, последние номера, спешите. The machine-assisted German, Italian and Russian terms are a minimum: QA may add terms, never remove them.

## Functional requirements

### Public site content and navigation

**FR-1 Page inventory.** The public site shall provide the following pages in each of the four languages, 21 per language and 84 in total: Home; Rooms and suites overview; one page for each of six room types (Family Suite, Executive Suite, Superior Twin Sea View, Superior Queen Sea View, Triple Deluxe Twin (pool view), Triple Deluxe Queen (pool view)); Dining; Pools and beach; Activities and facilities; Location; Offers; Gallery; FAQ; Contact Us; Privacy notice; Cookie notice; Terms; Accessibility statement; Newsletter unsubscribe. Each page shall have its own URL per language. The site shall have no currency selector, no meetings or events pages (OQ-18) and no site search page. Source: brief Scope In; Analyst (accessibility statement, unsubscribe page); OQ-18. Verify: T, I.

**FR-2 Rooms content.** The Rooms and suites page shall list all six room types, each as a card with name, one image, a short description and a link to its room page. Each room page shall show the room name, a description of at least 60 words, at least 3 images, a features list of at least 5 items and a Book now link (FR-11). Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T.

**FR-3 Dining content.** The Dining page shall present all ten venues named in the brief (Aqua Beach Restaurant, Oscar Restaurant, Italian Restaurant, Main Restaurant, French Cafe, Shisha Lounge, Lobby Bar, Lobby Lounge and Terrace, Twist Pool Bar, Splash Pool Bar), each with name, description of at least 25 words and one image, and each individually reachable by an anchor in the URL. Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T.

**FR-4 Pools, beach, activities and facilities content.** The Pools and beach page shall present four items (infinity pool, relaxing pool, kids' pool, private beach). The Activities and facilities page shall present five items (spa, gym, tennis court, kids' club and playground, entertainment). Each item shall have a name, a description of at least 25 words and one image. Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T.

**FR-5 Home content.** The Home page shall contain: the 3D hero (FR-27) with the resort name as page heading and a Book now link and a link to Contact Us; a resort overview section; a key facts section (qualitative facts only, see FR-18); a current offers summary showing the titles of the offers from the Offers page with a link to it; and links to Rooms, Dining, Pools and beach, Activities and facilities, Location and Gallery. Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T.

**FR-6 Location content.** The Location page shall show a static map image (no map tiles loaded from a third party at view time), a link labelled for opening the resort's position in Google Maps, and a list of at least 5 nearby attractions each with a name and a description of at least 15 words and no distances or travel times. Source: brief Scope In and Integrations (static map or link). Verify: T, I.

**FR-7 Offers content.** The Offers page shall list at least 3 offers, each with a title, a description of at least 25 words, the label "Sample offer" (in the page language) and a Book now link. No offer shall show a price, a discount amount, a percentage, a currency symbol, a date, a validity period, a deadline, a countdown, or any scarcity or urgency wording from the Forbidden claims list (for example "limited time", "last rooms", "only today"). Source: brief Scope In; Analyst (no prices, see D4); OQ-19; LR-29 (a false limited-time or availability claim is unfair in all circumstances). Verify: T.

**FR-8 Gallery.** The Gallery page shall show at least 12 images in a grid. Activating an image shall open a lightbox showing the large image, its caption, previous and next controls, a close control and an image counter. The lightbox shall be operable with keyboard alone (arrow keys for previous and next, Escape to close), shall keep keyboard focus inside while open, and shall return focus to the activating image on close. Every image shall have alternative text in each language. Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T, M.

**FR-9 FAQ.** The FAQ page shall list at least 8 questions, each expanding to show its answer. Each question shall be a button operable with Enter and Space, shall expose its expanded or collapsed state to assistive technology, and its answer shall be present in the page without JavaScript (readable when JavaScript is off). Source: brief Scope In. Verify: T.

**FR-10 Global navigation and footer.** Every public page shall have: a header with the resort name linking to Home, links to Rooms and suites, Dining, Pools and beach, Activities and facilities, Offers, Gallery, Location and Contact Us, a Book now link and a language switcher; a menu that collapses behind a button below 960 CSS pixels wide and opens and closes with keyboard and touch; the current page marked with `aria-current="page"`; and a footer with the newsletter sign-up form (FR-22), the contact links and social links (FR-12), links to Privacy notice, Cookie notice, Terms, Accessibility statement and Newsletter unsubscribe, and the concept notice (FR-17). Source: brief Scope In; Analyst (accessibility statement link). Verify: T.

**FR-11 Book now links.** Every Book now link on the site shall use one destination URL held as a single admin setting (FR-39), shall open in a new browser tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, and shall have an accessible name that includes the words "opens in a new tab" in the page language. Every Book now link shall be shown with a visible label in the page language, "External site, not connected with this concept", placed in the same element as the link or immediately after it, visible without hover or focus, in text of at least 14 CSS pixels that meets the contrast rules in NFR-4, and referenced from the link by `aria-describedby`. The site shall not collect, display or calculate availability, prices or payment details. Source: D4; OQ-15; brief Scope Out. Verify: T. Provisional (OQ-21) for the default destination.

**FR-12 Contact details and external links.** The site shall show, from admin settings (FR-39): a telephone number as a `tel:` link, a WhatsApp click-to-chat link of the form `https://wa.me/<number>`, an email address as a `mailto:` link, a postal address, a Google Maps link, and social links to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and YouTube. External links shall open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. A social or WhatsApp link with no value set shall not be rendered. The default values shall be synthetic placeholders: telephone `+44 20 7946 0000`, WhatsApp number `442079460001`, email `info@example.invalid`, a postal address line marked "placeholder", and social links of the form `https://social.example.invalid/<network>`; the Google Maps link shall point to the resort's public location. Source: brief Scope In and Integrations; OQ-16. Verify: T.

**FR-13 Error pages.** The site shall show a localised "page not found" page for any unknown URL (HTTP 404) and a localised "something went wrong" page for server errors (HTTP 500), each with the header, footer, concept notice, a link to Home and no technical detail (no stack trace, file path or software version). Source: Analyst. Verify: T.

### Legal pages and concept marking

**FR-14 Privacy notice content.** The Privacy notice shall state in each language: who the controller is and how to contact them (a clearly marked placeholder until the solicitor answers Q3); each purpose (answering enquiries, newsletter, admin accounts, security logging and rate limiting); the lawful basis for each purpose (for enquiries a basis other than consent, either steps at the person's request before a contract or legitimate interests, with the wording to be confirmed with the solicitor in Q7; for the newsletter, consent; for admin accounts and for security logging and rate limiting, the basis named in the notice) and, wherever legitimate interests is the basis, the legitimate interests pursued; the categories of data collected by each form; which form fields are required and what happens if they are not given; the right to withdraw consent at any time and how (the unsubscribe page); who receives the data; where it is stored and whether it leaves the country of the server; the retention period for each category (matching the configured values, FR-45); that data deleted from the live site can remain in backup archives for up to 35 days; the individual's rights and how to use them; how to complain to the controller about data protection (the Contact Us subject "Data protection request or complaint", FR-19, which the controller acknowledges within 30 days of receipt); the right to complain to the Information Commission (the regulator named in legislation from 30 September 2026, formerly the Information Commissioner's Office); and that the site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The notice shall be linked from the footer of every page and, by a link in the privacy information line or consent text, from every form. Source: D1; brief Data and legal flags; LR-2, LR-4, LR-5, LR-8. Verify: I, T. Provisional (OQ-12) for the controller and the enquiry basis.

**FR-15 Cookie and storage notice.** The Cookie notice shall list every cookie and every browser storage item the site can set, with name, purpose, category (strictly necessary or not), lifetime and which pages set it, in each language. The list shall match what the site actually sets: on the public site only the pause-motion choice in session storage (FR-30); in the admin portal only the session cookie (NFR-20). Public form tokens are held in the form and use no cookie or storage (NFR-22). Source: D1; brief Data and legal flags (PECR); LR-21. Verify: T, I.

**FR-16 Terms, who runs this site, accessibility statement and about this concept.** The Terms page shall include a section "About this concept" saying that the site is an unofficial, speculative design concept, is not operated, authorised or endorsed by JAZ Hotels or Jaz Elite Casa del Mar Beach, uses placeholder images and text, and does not accept bookings. The Terms page shall also include a section "Who runs this site" giving, from the settings in FR-39: the provider's legal name, geographic address, contact details including an email address, trade register name and number if any, supervisory authority if any, professional details if any, and VAT number if any; every value defaults to a marked placeholder ("to be confirmed"). The Accessibility statement shall state the target (WCAG 2.2 level AA), the date it was last reviewed, known limitations (empty if none) and a route to report a problem. A content register in the release package shall record, for each of the four legal pages (Privacy notice, Cookie notice, Terms, Accessibility statement) in German, Italian and Russian, whether a qualified person has reviewed the translation (initially: not reviewed, local review only). Source: D2; Analyst; OQ-8; LR-32. Verify: I, T.

**FR-17 Concept marking.** Every public page, error page, form result page and admin portal screen shall show a concept notice that: is placed in normal page flow at the top of the page (not fixed or sticky, so it can never cover page content or keyboard focus) and visible without scrolling on load, and is repeated in the footer; begins with the fixed concept sentence in the page language (held in the language resource files, not in the content store, so not editable; admin portal screens show the English sentence); may be followed by one optional plain text sentence of at most 200 characters per language, set in settings (FR-39), with no markup; has no control that hides, closes or collapses it and no focusable element inside it; and is at most 48 CSS pixels tall on screens narrower than 600 pixels when no optional sentence is set. The browser title of every page shall end with the title suffix, added by the system after the editable page title (FR-35), so that editing a title can never remove the suffix. No admin function shall change the fixed sentence or the title suffix. Source: D2; OQ-5; G-6 (TM-01, TM-37); M6; legal register LR-25, LR-33, LR-34. Verify: T, M.

**FR-18 Placeholder content rules.** All images, 3D models, 3D textures and text shall be original, generated, or licensed royalty free for commercial use, and none shall be copied from jazhotels.com. A licence register in the release package shall have an entry for every image, font and 3D input file (models, textures, HDR environment maps, materials and any generated asset), recording source, licence and date. Placeholder images shall not show identifiable real people. No page shall show a price, a currency amount, a star rating, an award, a certification, an eco or quality badge, a review quote or a review score, or a logo or badge of JAZ, Tripadvisor or any other third party; the resort name appears as typeset text only. No visible text shall contain a term from the Forbidden claims list. In the main content of every page other than the Privacy notice, Cookie notice, Terms, Accessibility statement, Newsletter unsubscribe and error pages (excluding header, footer, contact details, form hints and the gallery counter), digits may appear only as counts of items the page lists (4, 5, 6 or 10 as named in FR-2 to FR-4), list numbering and a copyright year; no size, distance, time, capacity or count of rooms shall be stated. The site shall not hotlink or request any asset from jazhotels.com. Source: D2; OQ-17; OQ-19; brief Content and brand; LR-39. Verify: T, I.

### Forms

**FR-19 Contact form fields and validation.** The Contact Us page shall provide a form with: name (required, 2 to 100 characters), email address (required, valid address format, at most 254 characters), telephone (optional, 7 to 20 characters from digits, spaces, plus, brackets and hyphens), subject (required choice of "General enquiry", selected by default, or "Data protection request or complaint"), message (required, 10 to 2000 characters), a privacy information line next to the send button stating that the details are used to answer the message and linking to the Privacy notice, a hidden spam trap field and a signed form token (FR-20, NFR-22). The form shall have no consent checkbox: answering an enquiry does not rely on consent (LR-2). The form shall also mark which fields are required. Validation shall run in the browser and again on the server, with identical rules. Error messages shall be in the page language, be shown next to each field and in a summary at the top of the form, and name the field and the problem. No other personal data shall be requested. Source: D1; brief Integrations; LR-2; LR-5. Verify: T.

**FR-20 Contact form submission.** On a valid submission the server shall store the enquiry (name, email, telephone if given, subject, message, language of the page, time received, and the version of the privacy information text shown), assign a reference of the form `ENQ-` followed by 6 characters from A to Z and 2 to 9, and show a confirmation page in the page language that states the reference and that the enquiry was received. A submission shall be rejected without storing it when the spam trap field is filled in, when the form token is missing, altered or older than 8 hours, or when it is received less than 2 seconds after the form was displayed. In every such rejection the visitor shall see the same visible message in the page language ("We could not send your message. Please check it and send it again."), the form shall be shown again with every typed value kept and focus moved to the message, and the message shall not reveal which test failed; a resubmission 2 seconds or more after the form was shown again shall be accepted. The form shall work with JavaScript turned off (a normal form post). When the server cannot store the enquiry the visitor shall see an error message, the typed values shall be kept in the form, and no confirmation shall be shown. Source: D1; brief Integrations; Analyst (spam trap and minimum time); M7 (WCAG 3.3.7). Verify: T.

### Motion and 3D

**FR-27 Immersive 3D hero.** The Home page shall open with a real-time 3D hero scene (a Red Sea seascape or resort scene, built by the agency) that renders in a canvas behind the hero text. The scene shall react to the visitor: pointer movement on devices with a mouse, touch drag on phones (without blocking vertical page scroll) and, where the browser offers it, device tilt. No function depends on the interaction. The hero heading, text and links shall be real page text, not drawn in the canvas. Until the scene has rendered its first frame, and in fallback mode, a static poster image of the scene shall be shown in the same place. Source: brief Motion appetite ("Immersive") and Goal; L5. Verify: T, M.

**FR-29 Loader and page transitions.** The site shall show: (a) a branded loader screen on the first visit to the site in a browser tab, only if the page is not ready within 300 ms (NFR-9), and only until the hero assets are ready; (b) a transition screen when moving between pages in the same tab, containing the resort name and a progress or motion indicator. **FR-31 3D asset authoring and delivery.** All 3D inputs, meaning models, textures, HDR environment maps, materials and any generated asset used in a scene, shall be created by the agency or covered by a licence recorded in the licence register (FR-18); no third-party model shall be used. ## Non-functional requirements

### Accessibility

**NFR-1 WCAG 2.2 level AA.** All public pages (all 84 URLs in the page inventory), the error pages, all form states (error, confirmation) and all admin portal screens shall meet every WCAG 2.2 level A and AA success criterion, in 3D mode and in fallback mode. Evidence shall be an automated scan with axe-core (version recorded) giving zero violations tagged `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21aa` or `wcag22aa` on every page and state, plus the manual checks in NFR-2 to NFR-5. Lighthouse accessibility score shall be at least 95 on every public page. Source: brief Data and legal flags; agency standard. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-2 Keyboard and focus.** Every function on the public site and in the admin portal shall be usable with a keyboard alone. Tab order shall follow reading order. A visible focus indicator of at least 2 CSS pixels with 3 to 1 contrast against adjacent colours shall show on every focusable element, and the focused element shall never be fully hidden by the concept notice, the sticky header or any consent control (WCAG 2.4.11). A "Skip to main content" link shall be the first focusable element on every page. No keyboard trap shall exist, including inside 3D scenes, the lightbox (which closes with Escape) and the menu. Pointer targets shall be at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels (WCAG 2.5.8). Every drag interaction shall have a non-drag alternative (WCAG 2.5.7). Source: WCAG 2.2. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-3 Screen reader support.** Pages shall use landmarks (header, nav, main, footer), exactly one `h1` per page, a heading order with no skipped levels, a language attribute on the page and on any text in another language, accessible names for all controls, status messages announced without moving focus (WCAG 4.1.3), and form errors tied to their fields. A 3D canvas shall be hidden from assistive technology (`aria-hidden="true"`), with the hero's meaning carried by real page text; informative images shall have alternative text and decorative images an empty `alt`. The journeys Home to Rooms to a room page, submit the contact form, subscribe to the newsletter, and (admin) sign in, edit a text, upload a photo shall be completed with NVDA on Windows with Firefox, and with VoiceOver on iOS with Safari, without sight. Source: WCAG 2.2; D2. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-4 Visual presentation.** Text contrast shall be at least 4.5 to 1 (3 to 1 for text of 24 CSS pixels or larger, or 19 pixels bold) and contrast of interface components and graphics at least 3 to 1. Text drawn over images, video or 3D shall meet this over every frame of a scene and on the poster image, by means of a solid or gradient backing, checked at the worst frame. Pages shall reflow without horizontal scrolling or loss of content at 320 CSS pixels wide, work at 200 percent zoom, and tolerate WCAG text spacing overrides without content clipping. Meaning shall not rely on colour alone. Body text shall be at least 16 CSS pixels. Source: WCAG 2.2. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-5 Authentication accessibility and time.** The admin sign in shall allow pasting into every field, allow password managers, and set `autocomplete` values (`username`, `current-password`, `one-time-code`), and shall not require solving a puzzle or transcribing characters (WCAG 3.3.8). Information the user has already given in the same process shall not be asked for again (WCAG 3.3.7). Any session timeout shall warn at least 2 minutes before it happens and allow extension (WCAG 2.2.1). Source: WCAG 2.2. Verify: T, M.

### Performance

**NFR-6 Core Web Vitals budgets.** On R1 or L1, cold cache, for each public page in each language, in 3D mode: Largest Contentful Paint at most 2.5 s; Cumulative Layout Shift at most 0.1; Total Blocking Time at most 300 ms; First Contentful Paint at most 1.8 s; and Interaction to Next Paint at most 200 ms measured over a scripted set of interactions (open menu, switch language, open and close a lightbox image, expand a FAQ item, type in a form field). Server response time for page HTML shall be at most 200 ms when the package runs on the machine used for review. The Lighthouse mobile Performance score shall be at least 80 in 3D mode and at least 90 in fallback mode, and the Best Practices score at least 95. (The Lighthouse SEO category is excluded because the concept is deliberately noindex.) Source: brief Constraints (mid range phones); agency budgets. Verify: A.

**NFR-7 Weight budgets.** Measured as compressed bytes transferred on first load of a page with a cold cache and no scrolling: all content needed before any 3D loads (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts and the largest above-the-fold image) at most 600 KB; JavaScript excluding the 3D chunk at most 200 KB; CSS at most 60 KB; web fonts at most 120 KB in total and at most 2 font families, with only the character subsets the page language needs (the Cyrillic file shall not be requested on en, de or it pages); the 3D code chunk (the 3D library and scene code) at most 350 KB; 3D assets (models and textures) for the Home hero at most 3 MB and for each other scene at most 2 MB; hero poster image at most 150 KB; every other delivered image file, including the posters of the five section scenes, at most 250 KB. 3D code and assets shall be requested only after the page's text and poster have rendered. Source: brief Constraints; agency budgets. Verify: A, T.

**NFR-8 3D runtime budget.** On R1 (or, when R1 is not available, on lab profile L2 with the same thresholds, the result labelled "emulated" and confirmation on R1 left as an open item for T3), over a scripted 20 second run of each scene including interaction: median frame rate at least 50 frames per second and the slowest 1 percent of frames no slower than 30 frames per second; no more than 150,000 triangles and no more than 100 draw calls in any frame; render resolution capped at a device pixel ratio of 1.5 on touch devices and 2 on others; first rendered frame within 4 s of navigation start on L1. If the median frame time over any 2 second window exceeds 22 ms, the scene shall reduce its quality by one step (render scale, effects, shadows), up to three steps; if after the lowest step the median frame time still exceeds 33 ms for 3 consecutive seconds, the page shall switch to fallback mode. Rendering shall stop within 1 second when the browser tab is hidden or the scene is off screen, and resume when it returns. Only one WebGL context shall exist at a time, and after 20 navigations between pages the JavaScript heap, measured after a forced garbage collection, shall have grown by at most 25 MB from its size after first load. Source: brief Motion appetite; agency budgets. Verify: A, T.

**NFR-9 Transition and loader timing.** On R1 or L1 a page transition (exit plus entry) shall take at most 800 ms when the next page is ready, shall block user input for no more than 100 ms in total, and shall not by itself increase the next page's Largest Contentful Paint by more than 200 ms. The initial loader shall appear only if the page is not ready within 300 ms, and shall never be visible for more than 8 seconds. Source: brief Goal; FR-29. Verify: A, T.

**NFR-10 Server and admin performance.** On a server of 1 CPU and 1 GB memory (OQ-14): public page HTML responds within 200 ms at the 95th percentile with 25 simultaneous visitors; admin read requests respond within 500 ms at the 95th percentile; the enquiries list shows its first page within 1 s when 5,000 enquiries exist; a 10 MB photo upload is processed (FR-41) within 30 s; a CSV export of 10,000 subscribers completes within 10 s. Source: Analyst; D6 (no running cost, so small servers). Verify: A.

### Reduced motion, fallback and no-JavaScript

**NFR-11 Reduced motion.** When the visitor's system asks for reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`), the site shall: not start any 3D scene (the poster image is shown instead), not start any parallax, scroll-driven animation, auto-playing animation or looping effect, replace page transitions and the loader with an instant swap or an opacity change of at most 150 ms, and not zoom, slide or spin any element. All content, links and functions shall remain available. The motion controls (FR-30) shall still be present where motion exists. Source: brief Motion appetite; WCAG 2.3.3. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-12 Non-WebGL and low-capability fallback.** The site shall switch to fallback mode, showing for each scene its static poster image (at most 150 KB for the hero poster and 250 KB for every other poster, as in NFR-7, with no layout shift against 3D mode) and no 3D code or 3D asset download, when any of these is true: the browser has no WebGL 2 support; creating the WebGL context fails; the context is lost and not restored within 3 seconds; the visitor asked for reduced motion (NFR-11); the connection reports data saving (`Save-Data`) or an effective type of 2g or slow-2g; the visitor has paused motion (FR-30); the adaptive rule in NFR-8 has triggered. In fallback mode every text, link, form and function of the page shall be present and behave exactly as in 3D mode, and the page shall pass NFR-1 and the fallback performance scores in NFR-6. Source: brief Motion appetite; design standard. Verify: T, M.

**NFR-13 Progressive enhancement.** With JavaScript turned off, every public page shall display all its text and images, navigation and language switching shall work, the contact form, newsletter sign-up and unsubscribe shall work by normal form submission, the FAQ answers shall be readable, and the concept notice shall be shown. Gallery lightbox, 3D, loader and transitions are the only functions allowed to be absent. The admin portal may require JavaScript. Source: Analyst; brief Constraints (graceful fallback). Verify: T.

### Privacy

**NFR-14 Data protection by design.** The site shall collect only the data fields listed in FR-19, FR-20, FR-22, FR-23, FR-32 to FR-34 and FR-46 and nothing else; shall use the data only for the purposes stated in the Privacy notice; shall record for every subscriber the wording version of the consent text agreed to and the time, and for every enquiry the version of the privacy information text shown; shall not pre-tick any consent box; shall not ask for consent to answer an enquiry (LR-2); shall not make any service conditional on newsletter consent; shall not ask for or infer anyone's age and shall not be directed at children; and shall not send personal data to any third party. Application logs shall not contain enquiry text, email addresses, passwords or one-time codes. Source: D1; brief Data and legal flags; LR-2. Verify: T, I. Provisional (OQ-12) for the lawful basis wording.

**NFR-15 Cookies, storage and third-party requests (PECR).** The public site shall set no cookie and write nothing to local or session storage before or without the visitor's explicit opt-in, except for items that are strictly necessary or that the visitor explicitly asked for: the pause-motion choice in session storage (FR-30), and the admin session cookie, which is set only on the admin portal. Public form tokens (NFR-22) are carried in the form itself and use no cookie and no storage. Each such item is named in the Cookie notice (FR-15). No analytics, advertising, social media widget, embedded video, embedded map or tag manager shall be present. Fonts, scripts, images and all other assets shall be served from the site's own origin: a page load shall make zero requests to any other host. If a non-essential cookie or storage item is ever added, a consent control shall first be added that: shows before the item is set, offers Accept and Reject with equal prominence, is off by default, lets the visitor change or withdraw the choice at any time from a link in the footer, and records nothing until accepted. Source: D1; brief Data and legal flags (PECR); D6; LR-21. Verify: T, I.

**NFR-16 Individual rights.** The site shall support, through FR-44, finding, exporting, correcting (by deleting and re-entering) and deleting all personal data held for an email address. A search shall take at most 5 s. The hosting guide and Privacy notice shall state that requests are answered within one calendar month and that a data protection complaint is acknowledged within 30 days of receipt. Source: D1; LR-5. Verify: T, I.

**NFR-17 Retention.** Personal data shall be kept no longer than the periods in the Retention schedule below. Backup archives created by FR-48 shall be kept no longer than 35 days by default and the backup command shall delete older archives it created. The Privacy notice, the hosting guide and the backup command's output shall state that data purged from the live store remains in backup archives until they expire. Source: D1; OQ-10; LR-8. Verify: T.

**NFR-18 Storage location and protection.** All personal data shall be stored only in the site's own storage on the server running the site. Passwords shall be stored only as salted hashes (NFR-19). Pages that collect personal data and the whole admin portal shall be served over HTTPS whenever the site is reachable from another machine. The package shall refuse to start on a non local address unless HTTPS is configured and two-step verification (FR-33) is built and enforced for every admin account; in this release FR-33 is not built, so the package refuses every non local address (G-22). A refusal shall exit with a non-zero status within 10 seconds, write a message beginning `STARTUP-REFUSED` that names the listen address setting, and leave no port listening. Uploaded originals, backup archives and the data store shall not be reachable by any public URL. Source: D1; D6; OQ-13; G-22. Verify: T, I.

### Security

**NFR-19 Admin authentication strength.** Passwords shall be at least 12 characters, with no maximum below 128 characters and no composition rules, shall be rejected if they appear in a bundled list of at least 10,000 common passwords or contain the account's email address, and shall be stored with Argon2id (memory at least 19 MiB, at least 2 iterations, 1 degree of parallelism) or, if Argon2id is unavailable on the server, bcrypt with cost at least 12. After 5 failed sign in attempts for one account within 15 minutes the account shall be locked for 15 minutes (the lock applies to the account whatever the source address, and the response shall not reveal whether an account exists). Response time for unknown and known accounts shall not differ by more than 100 ms at the median. Source: D3; Analyst. Verify: T, I.

**NFR-20 Sessions.** The admin session shall use a random identifier of at least 128 bits, held in a cookie marked `HttpOnly` and `SameSite=Strict` and, whenever the page is served over HTTPS, `Secure`; shall be replaced at sign in; shall end after 30 minutes without activity and 8 hours after sign in; shall end on sign out; and no admin response shall be cacheable (`Cache-Control: no-store`). All sessions of an account, including the one that made the change, shall end immediately when its password is changed or reset, or when the account is disabled or deleted. Source: D3; Analyst; G-19; L2. Verify: T.

**NFR-21 Rate limiting.** Priority: Must (promoted at T1, G-10). The server shall limit requests per client address and return HTTP 429 with a `Retry-After` header when exceeded: admin sign in at most 20 attempts per 15 minutes; contact form at most 5 submissions per hour; newsletter sign-up at most 5 per hour; newsletter unsubscribe at most 10 per hour; admin write requests at most 300 per minute per session; media uploads at most 30 per hour per user. Counters shall be kept no longer than 24 hours. The client address shall be taken from a forwarding header only when the direct peer is a configured trusted proxy; by default no forwarding header is trusted. Source: Analyst (security); D1; G-10; G-20. Verify: T.

**NFR-22 Request forgery protection.** Every request that changes state (all public forms, sign out, and every admin action) shall be accepted only with a valid anti-forgery token, and only when the `Origin` or `Referer` header, if present, matches the site's own origin. For public forms the token is a signed, tamper evident token carried in a hidden field of the form (it also carries the time the form was shown, FR-20) and uses no cookie and no storage; for the admin portal the token is tied to the session. State-changing actions shall never use the HTTP GET method. A request lacking a valid token shall be refused with HTTP 403 and shall change nothing, except that a public form with a missing, altered or expired token is handled as in FR-20 (visible retry message, typed values kept). Source: Analyst (security); LR-21; G-9. Verify: T.

**NFR-23 Upload validation.** The server shall accept an upload only from a signed-in user, only if its content (not its file name or declared type) is a JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF image, its size is at most 10 MB, its dimensions are at most 40 megapixels, and the batch has at most 20 files. Image dimensions shall be read from the file header and checked before the image is decoded, and every image shall be decoded and re-encoded in a separate worker or process with a time limit of 60 s and a memory limit of 256 MB; a failure shall reject the upload and leave the site serving. The server shall refuse every other type, including SVG, GIF, PDF, HTML and archives, and files whose extension disagrees with their content. Stored file names shall be random, the upload folder shall not run scripts, files shall be served with the correct media type and `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, and path characters in uploaded names shall never reach the file system. Total media storage shall have a configurable limit (default 2 GB) with a warning in the admin portal at 80 percent and refusal at 100 percent. Source: D3; Analyst; G-14. Verify: T.

**NFR-24 Injection and output safety.** All input shall be validated on the server by type, length and format; database access shall use parameterised queries only; all output shall be encoded for its context; formatted body text (FR-35) shall be passed through an allow-list sanitiser on save for every language value, keeping only paragraphs, bold, italic, links (with `https:`, `http:`, `mailto:` and `tel:` schemes only) and lists, and its output shall still be encoded on render; plain text fields shall reject bidirectional control characters (U+202A to U+202E and U+2066 to U+2069) and all other invisible format characters (Unicode category Cf) except the soft hyphen (U+00AD). The following headers shall be sent on every response of the public site, the error pages and the admin portal: `Content-Security-Policy` with `default-src 'self'`, no `unsafe-inline` and no `unsafe-eval` in `script-src` (`wasm-unsafe-eval` is allowed only if a WebAssembly decoder is used for 3D assets), `object-src 'none'`, `base-uri 'self'`, `form-action 'self'` and `frame-ancestors 'none'`; `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`; `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`; a `Permissions-Policy` that disables camera, microphone, geolocation and payment; and, over HTTPS, `Strict-Transport-Security` with a `max-age` of at least 15,552,000 seconds. Responses shall not carry server or framework version headers and the site shall not serve source maps. The admin portal shall show enquiry, subscriber and media metadata only as encoded text. No CSP violation shall be logged by the browser during the end-to-end tests. Source: Analyst (security); G-7; G-8. Verify: T, I.

**NFR-25 Authorisation.** Every admin route and every admin data request shall be refused with HTTP 401 (not signed in) unless the user is signed in; access shall be denied by default for routes with no rule; a user shall not be able to read or change a record by guessing its identifier (every record request returns 404 for a record that does not exist and is otherwise checked for sign in). There is one role in this release (OQ-1), so every signed-in account may use every admin function; separate roles return before real enquiries or subscribers exist. Source: D3; Analyst; OQ-1. Verify: T.

**NFR-26 Secrets, errors and dependencies.** No secret, key, password or personal data shall be committed to any repository or included in the release package other than synthetic examples; secrets shall be read from server configuration; error responses shall not expose stack traces, file paths, query text or software versions; a dependency audit (the package manager's built-in audit or equivalent) shall report no known vulnerability of severity high or critical in anything shipped, at the time of release; dependencies shall be pinned by a lock file; the release shall include a software bill of materials; dependency install scripts shall be disabled except for an approved list; any WebAssembly or worker decoder shall come from a pinned dependency and be served from the site's own origin; every `.glb` file shall pass the Khronos glTF validator with no errors and reference no external URI; and the configuration shall be secure by default (debug off, local address only). Source: Analyst; repo rules; G-18. Verify: T, I.

### Search engines, languages, browsers

**NFR-27 Noindex everywhere.** Every HTTP response that carries a page (public pages in all languages, error pages, form result pages, admin portal screens) shall carry both the header `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet` and the meta tag `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet">`. Image and file responses shall carry the header. The site shall serve no `sitemap.xml`, no canonical link and no alternate-language link pointing to the real JAZ domain, and no structured data (JSON-LD, microdata) describing the resort as a business or hotel. No admin setting shall change these rules. Source: D2. Verify: T.

**NFR-28 Internationalisation.** All user-visible text shall come from language resource files (which hold the fixed texts such as the fixed concept sentence and the title suffix) or the content store, never from code, in all four languages; at release 100 percent of public strings shall have a value in all four languages. Pages shall be encoded in UTF-8. Fonts shall contain every glyph used in the four languages, including the German letters ä ö ü ß, the Italian accented letters à è é ì ò ù and the Russian Cyrillic alphabet. Layouts shall show without clipped, overlapping or overflowing text when every string is 40 percent longer than its English version, at widths from 320 to 1920 CSS pixels. Form validation messages, confirmation messages and error pages shall be localised. Sorting and search in the admin portal shall treat Cyrillic and accented letters correctly. Source: D5. Verify: T, I.

**NFR-29 Browser, device and viewport support.** The public site shall work fully, in 3D mode where WebGL 2 is available and in fallback mode otherwise, in: the latest 2 stable major versions of Chrome, Edge and Firefox (Windows, macOS; Chrome also on Android); Safari 16.4 and later on macOS, iOS and iPadOS; and the latest 2 stable versions of Samsung Internet. It shall work at every viewport width from 320 to 2560 CSS pixels in portrait and landscape. The admin portal shall work in the desktop and tablet versions of the same browsers at widths from 768 CSS pixels. In other browsers the site shall still show its text and links. Source: brief Constraints (agency default). Verify: T, M.

### Cost, operations, brand

**NFR-30 Brand.** The design shall use brand elements recorded by the designer from the current site (theme colour #453536, the tone of short upbeat labels, and any fonts and palette found), together with a written list of what was extracted and what was enhanced for the resort and sector; no logo artwork, badge or image shall be copied (FR-18). The brand colours and type scale shall be defined once as design tokens and every colour pairing in use shall meet NFR-4. Source: D7. Verify: I.

**NFR-31 Zero running cost.** Operating the site shall need no paid third-party service, no subscription, no account with a third-party provider and no per-use fee. All software shipped or required at run time shall be licensed under a licence on the project's allow-list (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, 0BSD, CC0-1.0, MPL-2.0, SIL OFL-1.1 for fonts), and any other licence needs written approval recorded in the licence register. The release package shall contain a generated third party notices file listing every shipped dependency, font and 3D tool output with its licence name, copyright notice and, where the licence requires it, the licence text (LR-40). The site shall store content, media, enquiries and subscribers in its own storage, send no email in this release (OQ-2; if FR-21 is reinstated, only through the client's own SMTP host), and make no request to any external host while running (apart from links a visitor follows). The full test suite of the visitor and admin journeys shall pass when the machine's outgoing network is blocked entirely. The site shall run in 512 MB of memory at rest and in 1 GB under the load in NFR-10, on one CPU. Source: D6; brief Constraints; OQ-2; OQ-14; LR-40. Verify: T, I, A.

**NFR-32 Operability.** All settings (listening address and port, data folder, retention periods, session limits, upload limits, and the SMTP host and credentials if FR-21 is reinstated) shall be read from one documented configuration file or environment variables, with an example file containing no secrets. The configuration file and data folder shall be readable and writable only by the account running the site, and the site shall refuse to start if they are readable by other accounts. Signing keys shall be generated randomly per install at first run (at least 256 bits) and never shipped in the package. The package shall provide a health check that returns HTTP 200 only when the data store is readable and writable. The documented install and first run shall take no more than 10 commands. Source: Analyst; brief Constraints (hosting by the client, hosting guide before release); G-17. Verify: T, I.

**NFR-33 Data integrity.** An enquiry, subscriber record or content change that the site has confirmed to the user shall survive an abrupt stop of the software (power loss, process kill) immediately afterwards. Content saves shall be all-or-nothing: an interrupted save shall leave the previous content intact. Two staff saving the same page shall not silently overwrite each other: the second save shall be refused with a message and the chance to review the other change. Source: Analyst. Verify: T.

The record shall be kept for 12 months (Retention schedule), shall never contain passwords, one-time codes, session identifiers, enquiry text or subscriber email addresses, and shall not be editable or deletable through the admin portal. Source: G-13 (TM-06, TM-08, TM-40, TM-49, TM-66, TM-67). Verify: T.

## Retention schedule

Proposed by the analyst, extended after the UK legal review (`docs/legal-register.md`, LR-8) and approved by Steven at T1 (OQ-10). Every period is a configuration value (FR-45). Data removed from the live store can remain in backup archives until those archives expire (35 days, NFR-17); the Privacy notice says so (FR-14).

| Data | Default retention | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enquiries | 12 months after receipt | Permanently deleted |
| Record of a data subject request or data protection complaint (reference, type, dates received, acknowledged and closed, outcome; no message text) | 24 months after closure | Permanently deleted |
| Active newsletter subscriber | Until the person unsubscribes or an Administrator removes the record | Moves to the next two rows |
| Unsubscribed subscriber, address in readable form | 30 days after unsubscribing | Address deleted |
| Unsubscribed subscriber, suppression entry (a keyed hash of the address, so the address cannot be read back) | 36 months after unsubscribing | Entry deleted |
| Admin account personal data (name, email) after deletion of the account | 30 days | Deleted; log entries show "deleted user" |
| Admin account with no sign in | Disabled after 12 months | Administrator decides on deletion |
| Admin session data held by the server | Until sign out, 30 minutes idle or 8 hours after sign in, whichever comes first | Deleted at session end |
| Audit log (FR-46) | 12 months | Deleted |
| Security event record (NFR-34) | 12 months | Deleted |
| Rate limit counters | 24 hours | Deleted |
| Uploaded originals of photos deleted from the media library | 30 days after deletion from the library; public copies are removed at once | Deleted |
| Photos showing identifiable people | Placeholder images shall not show identifiable real people (FR-18); if staff upload one, it follows the row above and is kept only while used on the site | Deleted |
| CSV exports of subscribers | The site keeps no copy. The downloaded file is outside the site's control; the hosting guide tells staff to delete it after use | Deleted by staff |
| Web server access logs | 30 days (set by the host; the hosting guide says how) | Deleted |
| Backup archives | 35 days | Deleted by the backup command |
